Common questions

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?
Yes, we collect card details upfront to prevent abuse, but you won't be charged until your 14-day trial ends. Cancel any time before then and pay nothing.
Which sanctions lists are covered?
We screen against OFAC SDN, EU Consolidated List, UN Security Council, and UK FCDO — updated continuously. Enterprise plans include additional custom data feeds.
Can I change plans?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade any time from the billing portal. Upgrades are prorated immediately; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
How do I cancel?
Click "Manage Billing" in your dashboard — you'll land on the Stripe portal where you can cancel in one click. You keep access until the end of the billing period.
Is my payment data secure?
Card details are handled entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your payment information. Stripe is PCI DSS Level 1 certified.
